Shipping & delivery

When will my order arrive?

Most orders leave our little packing bench within 1–3 business days. Here is what happens after that.

We pack orders Monday through Friday from our workshop in Portland, Oregon. Standard domestic delivery usually takes 3–6 business days after dispatch. During a seasonal release, packing can take a little longer.

The short version

  • Order received: an email confirmation.
  • Packing: usually 1–3 business days.
  • Transit: usually 3–6 business days in the contiguous U.S.

These are estimates, not guarantees. Weather and carrier volume can affect the timeline. Your tracking page is the best source once a parcel is on its way.
