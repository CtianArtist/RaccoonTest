Privacy information

How information is handled when you browse or contact the desk.

Contact and support

When you use support chat, your message and details are handled by our support service to respond to your request. Please do not share passwords or full payment card numbers.

Support chat and cookies

The support service may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when it loads, and may use cookies to operate the conversation.

Your shopping bag

Your bag is stored in this browser tab. It contains product references and quantities and does not contain payment information.

Hosting and fonts

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. These providers may receive standard request information when you visit. We do not add a separate analytics service.

Questions

Contact the support desk if you have a question about information shared in a conversation.
