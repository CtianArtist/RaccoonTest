Shipping & delivery

When will my order arrive?

Most orders leave our little packing bench within 1–3 business days. Here is what happens after that.

We pack orders Monday through Friday from our workshop in Portland, Oregon. Standard domestic delivery usually takes 3–6 business days after dispatch. During a seasonal release, packing can take a little longer.

The short version

  • Order received: an email confirmation.
  • Packing: usually 1–3 business days.
  • Transit: usually 3–6 business days in the contiguous U.S.

These are estimates, not guarantees. Weather and carrier volume can affect the timeline. Your tracking page is the best source once a parcel is on its way.

Shipping & delivery

How do I track my order?

A tracking link appears in the dispatch email when your parcel leaves our bench.

When your order is dispatched, the dispatch email includes a carrier link and tracking number. It can take up to 24 hours for the first scan to appear.

No scan yet?

If the link says “label created,” give it one business day for the first carrier scan. If there is still no movement after 48 hours, contact support with your order number.

Orders

Can I change my address before dispatch?

Sometimes. Contact us quickly and we will try to catch the order before it is packed.

Contact support with your order number and the updated shipping address. Address changes are possible while an order is still unfulfilled.

Once a dispatch email has been sent, a change cannot be guaranteed. We will explain the next available step.

Returns & refunds

What is your returns policy?

Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

We accept returns of unused, unwashed items in their original condition within 30 days of delivery.

Return steps

  1. Contact support with your order number, item, and reason for return.
  2. Wait for return authorization and packing instructions.
  3. Pack the item securely with your order reference and keep your tracking details.

Original shipping charges are not refundable. Personalized or clearly used items are not eligible. Exchanges are handled as a return followed by a new order.

Returns & refunds

When will my refund arrive?

Approved refunds are processed within 3 business days after a return is received.

After a return reaches our workshop, we inspect it and process the approved refund within 3 business days. Your payment provider may then take another 5–10 business days to show the credit.

If that window has passed, contact support with your order number and return tracking details.

Orders

Can I cancel my order?

Cancellation is possible only before the order enters packing.

Contact support as soon as possible with your order number so we can check its fulfillment status.

If packing has started, cancellation may no longer be possible. You can ask about a return after delivery instead.

Orders

Why is my order taking longer to process?

Small-batch packing can run a little behind during launches, restocks, and rainy weeks.

Our packing bench usually works through orders in 1–3 business days. Processing can stretch to 5 business days during a new collection, a restock, or an unexpected supply delay.

If your order has been processing longer than 5 business days, contact support with your order number. We will check its status and explain the next step.
