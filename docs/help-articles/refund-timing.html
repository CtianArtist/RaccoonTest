Returns & refunds

When will my refund arrive?

Approved refunds are processed within 3 business days after a return is received.

After a return reaches our workshop, we inspect it and process the approved refund within 3 business days. Your payment provider may then take another 5–10 business days to show the credit.

If that window has passed, contact support with your order number and return tracking details.
