Shipping & delivery

When will my order arrive?

Demo orders are packed Monday through Friday at the fictional workshop in Portland, Oregon. Packing usually takes 1–3 business days. Standard domestic delivery usually takes another 3–6 business days after dispatch in the fictional contiguous U.S. Seasonal launches, weather, carrier volume, or supply delays can extend these estimates. The tracking page is the best source once a parcel is on its way.

Order confirmation is immediate in the demo storefront. Delivery estimates are not guarantees.

How do I track my order?

A fictional carrier link and tracking number appear in the dispatch email. It can take up to 24 hours for the first scan. If the status says “label created,” wait one business day for the first carrier scan. If there is still no movement after 48 hours, contact support with the order number. Demo tracking numbers are not connected to a live carrier.

Orders

Can I change my address before dispatch?

In a support-flow test, use the Zendesk widget with a sample order number and address. An address could only be updated while a real order was still unfulfilled; this demo has no real orders to change. Once a dispatch email has been sent, a change cannot be guaranteed. Do not include real addresses or payment details in a test message.

Can I cancel my order?

Cancellation is possible only before the order enters packing. Contact support as soon as possible and include the order number so the fulfillment status can be checked. If packing has started, cancellation may no longer be possible; you can ask about a return after delivery. This storefront does not create real orders or collect payment.

Why is my order taking longer to process?

The fictional packing bench usually processes orders in 1–3 business days. Processing can take up to 5 business days during a new collection, restock, or unexpected supply delay. If an order has been “processing” for longer than 5 business days, contact support with the order number to ask for its status and next step.

Returns & refunds

What is your returns policy?

Unused, unwashed items in their original condition may be returned within 30 days of delivery under the demo policy. Contact support with the order number, item, and a short reason to request a fictional return authorization. Pack the item securely with the order reference and share fictional tracking details when available.

Original shipping charges are not refundable. Personalized or clearly used items are not eligible. Exchanges are handled as a return followed by a new demo order. Checkout is not enabled in this environment.

When will my refund arrive?

After a return reaches the fictional workshop, it is inspected and the refund is marked within 3 business days. The payment provider may then take another 5–10 business days to show the credit. This is a safe content demo: no payment is collected and no real refund can be issued.

Account & contact

Account help and sign-in questions

Raccoon Supply Co. is a frontend-only testing environment. It has no customer accounts, password reset, saved cart, or order history. For a support-flow test, describe a fictional scenario in the Zendesk widget. Never enter a real password, payment card, or other sensitive information.

How can I contact support?

Open the Zendesk support widget on this page to ask a question. Messages entered there are handled by Zendesk, not by a local demo chat. This fictional store has no real orders or customer support team; use sample information only. For self-service answers, browse this help center.

Privacy information

This is a fictional test store with no real accounts, orders, checkout, or payments. There is no separate local chat or contact form. The Zendesk widget loads on every page and may send technical information to Zendesk; if you use it, your message may also be sent.
